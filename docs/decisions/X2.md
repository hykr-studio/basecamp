# X2: neutral tenant defaults (time zone, privacy contact)

## Summary

Two market values sat in the framework's `TenantSettings` as every business's defaults: the time
zone `Asia/Kolkata` and the privacy contact `privacy@example.in`. The branch's new mechanisms
compute business periods from the zone (number series years and financial years, business-hours
SLA due dates, summary date buckets, credit periods and caps, document dates, calendar invites,
agent schedules), so a deployment in Europe still rolled numbers and reset credits at Kolkata
midnight, and every business's privacy register and WhatsApp HELP printed a placeholder address
as its fiduciary contact.

Now neither has a framework value. A business's zone is its own `timeZone` setting, else the
deployment's `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE`, else UTC, resolved by one helper (`tenantZone` in
`@hykr/core`) at every site, so the fallbacks no longer disagree (Kolkata in some places, UTC in
others). A zone is UTC or an Area/Location name that Intl and Postgres both know and read alike,
never an offset or an abbreviation (Postgres reads `+05:30` and `IST` as other zones), because
some periods are computed in JS and some in SQL. A business sets its zone with
`PUT /api/settings/time-zone`, which checks it against Postgres too. The privacy contact is the
business's own `grievanceContact`, else the deployment's `DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT`, else none:
the register shows `null` and HELP says no contact is named and how to reach the team. New
WhatsApp contacts take the business's zone, and the channel tables' defaults are neutral. The
sample declares its India setup in `application/.env.example` (`DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE=Asia/Kolkata`,
a sample contact), next to `DEFAULT_CURRENCY=INR`.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `TenantSettings.timeZone` is optional, without a default. A stored value that is not a zone this process can use (decision 4: an offset, an abbreviation, a name Intl or Postgres does not know) is ignored, so the business follows the deployment's zone in JS and in SQL alike, rather than failing the business's settings: a bad setting never stops a write, as SLA settings already behave. | Task (review fix); B6B4's rule that a bad setting falls back; fix round 1 (a stored '+0530' made every SQL period a 500). |
| 2 | `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` (UTC or an IANA Area/Location name) is the deployment's zone for a business that set none. `@hykr/api`'s config reads it and `assertConfig` refuses to start on one `isTimeZone` refuses (`IST`, `+05:30`, `+0530`, an unknown name); it reaches core through `CoreOptions.defaultTimeZone` and `setDefaultTimeZone`, as `DEFAULT_CURRENCY` does. At start, `CoreModule`'s `TimeZonesCheck` (API, worker and CLI alike) reads the names Postgres knows and checks the deployment's zone with `zoneName`: one Postgres does not read as Intl does stops the start, and Postgres's spelling is kept. Unset: UTC. | Task ("follow the pattern DEFAULT_CURRENCY already uses"); B3B5 decision 5 (contracts never read the environment); fix round 1 ("apply the same check in assertConfig/setDefaultTimeZone"). |
| 3 | One resolver in `@hykr/core`: `tenantZone(settings)` (the business's own zone, else `defaultTimeZone()`), and `tenantZoneOf(conn, tenantId)`, which reads only the `timeZone` key in the caller's transaction, so another section's bad value never changes a zone. Every business-period site uses one of them: `tenantMoney` (number series, `GET /api/money`), `slaConfig`, summary buckets, credit periods (the deployment-only fallback plan too), document dates, calendar invites and free slots, agent schedules and definitions, quiet hours and notification readers. The local copies (`zoneOf`, `knownZone ?? 'UTC'`, `tenantTimeZone`, the agents' `setZoneOf` hook) are gone. | Task ("one `tenantZone(settings)` helper at every site, so the fallbacks stop disagreeing"). |
| 4 | A zone is a name JS (Intl) and SQL (Postgres's `AT TIME ZONE`) read the same way: some business periods are computed in each. `isTimeZone(value)` (`@hykr/contracts`, pure `Intl`, so the apps can use it) is the one shape check: UTC or an Area/Location name (`/^(UTC\|[A-Za-z]+(\/[A-Za-z0-9_+-]+)+)$/`, at most 64 characters) that Intl knows and whose Intl name is not an offset. Offsets and abbreviations are refused although Intl takes them: Postgres refuses `+0530`, reads `+05:30` with the POSIX sign (UTC-05:30) and `IST` as Israel's (+02:00). On the server, a usable zone is also a name Postgres knows (read once at start); `zoneName(conn, value)` (`@hykr/core`) adds the full check for a write: a name in `pg_timezone_names` (matched without case) whose offset from UTC is the same in Intl and Postgres now and six months on (their tz databases are different files, maybe of different releases), and gives Postgres's spelling. | Default: the same check was copied in six files; fix round 1 (review: Intl and Postgres disagree on offsets and abbreviations). |
| 5 | A person's zone, when the caller does not say it, is their business's: a chat turn or a resumed run without a valid `timeZone`, and a WhatsApp linking code sent without one (`POST /api/channels/whatsapp/code`, whose `timeZone` is now optional). Before: UTC in the first two, Kolkata in the third. | Task (link.controller falls back to Kolkata); default: the business's zone is the best guess for its people. |
| 6 | A WhatsApp contact the framework creates (a first message, the routing hook, a party's person) gets its business's zone when it is created. The column defaults become neutral: `channel.contacts.time_zone` `'UTC'`, `channel.contacts.locale` `'en'` (a language tag: the region was a market assumption, and only the language is read), `channel.link_codes.time_zone` `'UTC'`. Existing rows keep their values. The migration is `ALTER COLUMN … SET DEFAULT` only: no drop, rename or type change. | Task ("contacts table defaults … move these to the same deployment default"); migration rule (additive; the integrator rejects only `DROP` and `ALTER … TYPE`). |
| 7 | The inbound pipeline stores the language a contact writes in as the language alone (`hi`), not `hi-IN`. | Default: same class as 6 (a region appended for every market). `langOf` reads both. |
| 8 | `grievanceContact` is optional, without a default. The business's own contact wins; else `DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT` (optional, trimmed, 3 to 320 characters or the start is refused); else none. `fiduciary.grievanceContact` on the register and `grievanceContact` in `GET /api/privacy/settings` are `string \| null`. The resolver (`grievanceContactOf`, `features/privacy/contact.ts`) reads the variable on first use, not when the module loads: the privacy service is exported from `@hykr/api`'s index, and reading the config there would freeze it before a test (or a launcher) sets the environment. | Task ("make grievanceContact optional/null"; "a sample grievance contact … to application/.env.example"); plan boundary table (legal-basis and contact text are the business's). |
| 9 | WhatsApp HELP with no contact uses its own phrase, `channel.helpNoContact`: the same commands, then that no contact for data questions is named yet and "talk to a person" reaches the team. The back office's register says "no privacy contact named yet". | Task ("the register and HELP then say none is set"). |
| 10 | A business sets its own zone: `PUT /api/settings/time-zone { timeZone: string \| null }` (`settings.timeZone`, `settings.manage`, people only, no tool), through `runWrite`, audited with the before and after, `settings.changed` after commit. A zone `zoneName` refuses (an offset, an abbreviation, a name Intl or Postgres does not know or reads differently) is 400 `time_zone_invalid`; null goes back to the deployment's. The zone is stored as Postgres spells it (`europe/berlin` is stored `Europe/Berlin`). It answers `{ timeZone, effective }`; `GET /api/money` also gives the effective zone. | Default: with a neutral default a business outside the deployment's zone needs a way to set its own; modelled on `settings.branding`; fix round 1 (the check and the stored spelling). |
| 11 | The sample declares its setup: `application/.env.example` sets `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE=Asia/Kolkata` and `DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT=privacy@example.in` beside `DEFAULT_CURRENCY=INR`. The sample's tests that depend on a zone set the business's zone themselves, as they already set its currency. | Task ("add DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE=Asia/Kolkata (and a sample grievance contact …) to application/.env.example"); B3B5 decision 7. |
| 12 | Framework tests set the deployment defaults they depend on (`setDefaultTimeZone`, `setDefaultGrievanceContact`) and put them back, so a developer's `.env` (copied from the sample's) does not change their outcome. | Default: tests must not depend on the environment. |
| 13 | The stored name is Postgres's spelling of the name given, not Intl's canonical name. Node 24's Intl canonicalises to the old link names (`Asia/Kathmandu` to `Asia/Katmandu`, `Europe/Kyiv` to `Europe/Kiev`, `Pacific/Kanton` to `Pacific/Enderbury`), which a business would not recognise and a Postgres built on a system tz database without the backward links may not know. Intl's canonical name is still checked (it must not be an offset), and both runtimes read the stored name as the same zone (decision 4). | Fix round 1 (review asked to store the Intl canonical name; its purpose, a name both read alike, is kept with a name the business gave). |

## Assumptions to review

| Topic | Choice | Question for the product owner |
|---|---|---|
| A deployment-wide privacy contact | `DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT` is shown for every business that names none. The PRD makes each business the fiduciary and the platform a processor, so a multi-tenant deployment should leave it unset. | Should a multi-tenant deployment refuse `DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT`, or should onboarding require each business to name its contact before it goes live? |
| An invalid stored zone | Ignored (the deployment's zone applies) rather than refused: an offset or an abbreviation too, so JS and SQL agree. | Should the back office warn a business whose stored zone is not a zone? |
| Abbreviations and offsets | Refused everywhere (`IST`, `CET`, `GMT`, `+05:30`), and only `UTC` itself among the short names; a person picks an Area/Location name. | Should the settings screen offer a list of zones (and map a market's usual abbreviation to its zone) instead of free text? |
| Changing a business's zone | Allowed at any time to `settings.manage`; numbers, credit periods and SLA clocks already started keep their periods, the next ones follow the new zone. | Should a zone change mid-financial-year need an approval or be refused once numbers have been issued? |
| A contact's zone | A new contact takes its business's zone; a person who links their number gives their device's. | Should a contact's zone follow later changes to the business's zone? |

## API sketch

```text
@hykr/contracts   isTimeZone(value: unknown): value is string   (UTC or Area/Location, Intl knows it)
                  TenantSettings.timeZone?: string; TenantSettings.grievanceContact?: string
                  SetTimeZoneSpec ('settings.timeZone', PUT /api/settings/time-zone)
                    input { timeZone: string | null } → BusinessTimeZone { timeZone: string | null, effective: string }
                  PrivacyRegister.fiduciary.grievanceContact: string | null
                  PrivacyConfiguration.grievanceContact: string | null
@hykr/core        setDefaultTimeZone(zone?), defaultTimeZone(): string
                  zoneName(conn, value): Promise<string | null>   (Postgres's spelling, same offsets)
                  checkTimeZones(conn)   (at start, CoreModule's TimeZonesCheck)
                  tenantZone(settings?: { timeZone?: unknown } | null): string
                  tenantZoneOf(conn, tenantId): Promise<string>
                  CoreOptions.defaultTimeZone?: string
@hykr/api         env DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE, DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT (config, checked by assertConfig)
                  grievanceContactOf(settings): string | null; setDefaultGrievanceContact(contact?)
                  (testing-internals)
                  POST /api/channels/whatsapp/code { phone, timeZone?, lang? }
i18n              channel.helpNoContact (en, hi, te)
db                channel.contacts.time_zone default 'UTC', locale default 'en';
                  channel.link_codes.time_zone default 'UTC'
```

## Test plan

- `@hykr/core` unit (`src/tenants/time-zone.test.ts`): `isTimeZone` (IANA zones in any case,
  legacy and Etc names, UTC; refuses `IST`, `+05:30`, `+0530`, `-03`, `EST`, `CET`, `GMT`,
  `EST5EDT`, unknown names, non-strings, empty, too long); `tenantZone` (own zone; none, unknown,
  an offset or an abbreviation, non-string: the deployment's; no deployment zone: UTC);
  `setDefaultTimeZone` refuses an unknown zone, `IST`, `+05:30` and `+0530` and keeps the one it
  had, and clears on empty; `zoneName` (Postgres's spelling, null for a name Postgres lacks or
  reads with another offset, no query for an offset or an abbreviation); `checkTimeZones` (keeps
  Postgres's spelling, stops the start, then ignores a zone Postgres does not know).
- Fixture e2e `framework/api/test/framework/tenant-defaults.e2e.test.ts`:
  - a business without a zone follows the deployment's (`GET /api/money`), its own zone wins, an
    unknown stored zone falls back; a credit period starts at the business's midnight (Tokyo,
    Honolulu, UTC);
  - a stored `IST`, `+05:30` or `+0530` is ignored: the deployment's zone, and `GET /api/credits`
    answers 200 with the period at the deployment's midnight;
  - `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE` of `IST`, `+05:30` or `+0530` stops a process at start (`assertConfig`,
    in a child process); `Europe/Berlin` starts;
  - `PUT /api/settings/time-zone`: sets, clears (null), refuses an unknown or empty zone and
    `IST`, `+05:30`, `+0530` (400 `time_zone_invalid`), stores `europe/berlin` as `Europe/Berlin`,
    refuses staff without `settings.manage` (403, audited `denied`) and the assistant
    (`humans_only`), audits the command and the business's before and after (`tenant.update`),
    and reaches only its own business;
  - the register and `GET /api/privacy/settings` show `null` with no contact, the deployment's
    when it is set, the business's own over it, and the deployment's again once cleared.
- In the features' own suites: `money.e2e` (a number taken at 20:00 UTC on 31 March is FY2027 in a
  Tokyo deployment, FY2026 in UTC), `summary.e2e` (a month bucket follows the deployment's zone, and a stored offset or
  abbreviation is ignored, never a 500),
  `parties.e2e` (a party's person gets the business's zone), `whatsapp.e2e` (a first message's
  contact gets the business's zone and the `en` locale; a linking code without a zone links in
  the business's; HELP names the business's contact, the deployment's, or says none is named),
  `seams.e2e` (parsed settings carry no zone and no contact).
- The whole suite (`wt.sh test X2`) passes with no deployment defaults; the zone and contact
  suites (tenant-defaults, whatsapp, privacy, money, summary, seams, calendar, agents, credits,
  sla, notifications, documents, parties, settings) pass again with `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE=Asia/Kolkata`
  and `DEFAULT_GRIEVANCE_CONTACT` set, as a developer's `.env` from the sample has them.
- Fix round 1, as run: `@hykr/core` unit (15 files, 131 tests); `@hykr/api` e2e (67 files, 1062
  tests) and `@application/api` e2e (27 files, 1 skipped) with no deployment defaults; the
  tenant-defaults, summary and money suites again with `DEFAULT_TIME_ZONE=Asia/Kolkata`.
