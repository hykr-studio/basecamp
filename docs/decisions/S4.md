# S4: embeddings under credits (metered, priced and capped)

## Summary

A probe found that a business paused by its operator (`credits.override { mode: 'pause' }`) was
refused every assistant turn (402 `credits_suspended`) but `GET /api/memory/recall?q=…` still
answered 200 and called the embedding model, and the memory indexer kept embedding every
memory-trait write, backfill and reindex. Nothing was recorded either: the credits feature had no
meter for embeddings, so embedding tokens priced in `MODEL_PROVIDERS` were never charged or capped.
The recall route needs no capability, so any member, customer or the assistant could make one
embedding per call.

S4 puts embeddings under credits like every other costly use:

- A fifth framework meter, `embedding_tokens` (tokens, priced per million, the embedding model's
  ref as variant), priced by the plan or the deployment, else the model's own `MODEL_PROVIDERS`
  price plus the markup.
- Recall checks the meter before it calls the provider. Refused: `GET /api/memory/recall` (and so
  `recall-memory`) answers 402 `credits_paused` with the rule and reason; a turn runs without
  recall, as it does when recall cannot run for any other reason.
- The indexer checks once per business per batch. Refused: that business's rows stay `pending`,
  unclaimed, with no attempt counted, and are looked at again a few seconds later; other
  businesses' rows go on being embedded.
- Every embedding call that answers records its tokens (`source` `memory.recall` or
  `memory.index`), as the provider reported them, else estimated.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Embeddings get a **meter of their own**, `embedding_tokens` (`unit` token, `per` 1,000,000, variant the embedding model's ref), in `FrameworkMeters` and contributed by the credits feature like the other four. Not `tokens` with the embedding model as variant: a deployment's `tokens` price is a chat price (an embedding costs a small fraction of it), a `tokens` limit would then count embeddings against the assistant's turns, and the credits screen would show both as one number. | Task notes (either was allowed); default: prices and limits stay per kind of use. |
| 2 | The meter **pauses always** (no `pausable`): the operator's pause, the budget at `pauseAt` and its own `limits.embedding_tokens` refuse it. A limit on `tokens` or `tool_calls` does not, so a business can cap turns and keep recall, or the other way round. | A3 decisions 9 and 11 (a limit stops only its meter; a pause stops what costs money); PRD Platform & AI ("Credits and caps … pause at 100%"). |
| 3 | **Price.** In order: the plan's or deployment's `embedding_tokens.<model ref>`, then `embedding_tokens`, then the model's own `inputPerMillion` (`MODEL_PROVIDERS`) plus `markupPercent` when its currency is the credits currency, else nothing. The last step is a new optional `MeterDef.fallbackPrice(variant, plan)`, which `CreditsService.record` asks when no price is set, so any feature recording `embedding_tokens` (or a module's meter of its own) is priced without computing a cost itself. | A3 decision 4 (the same order for `tokens`); A10 decision 7 ("an unpriced model has no cost; A3 decides"). |
| 4 | `RecallService.embed` chooses the model (the policy), then asks `metering()?.check({ tenantId, meter: 'embedding_tokens', variant })`, then calls the provider. A refusal throws `UsageRefused` (`metering.ts`, holding the `UsageRefusal`). With the credits feature off (no meter in the slot) nothing is checked or recorded, as for every other meter. | Task notes; A3 decision 16 (the services reach the meter through the slot). |
| 5 | `GET /api/memory/recall` answers a refusal with **402** `{ error: 'credits_paused', rule, reason }`, the voice session's shape, rather than 200 with `model: null`: `model: null` means "this business has no embedding model it may use", and a person or the assistant must learn that credits stopped it and what fixes it. The `recall-memory` tool gets it as a refused API call (its `reason`). | Task notes ("402 or model: null"); A3 decision 13 (402 says what fixes it). |
| 6 | A **turn** whose recall is refused runs without recall and keeps nothing for conversation memory, like a recall that is off, refused by the policy or times out. The turn itself is the `credits` turn hook's to refuse, by the `tokens` and `tool_calls` meters. | A8 decision 8 ("recall only adds context"); A3 decision 11. |
| 7 | A credits check that fails (the database) is not a pass: the recall API answers 503 `recall_unavailable`, a turn runs without recall, and the indexer leaves the rows pending. Settings that do not parse are already a refusal (`credits_unavailable`, A3 decision 25). | Default, fail closed. |
| 8 | **The indexer** checks the meter once per business in each batch, before it loads any text. Refused (or the check failed): the business's claimed rows are released as they were (state `pending`, attempts, error and `updated_at` unchanged, claim cleared), and the business is skipped by the claim query for 5 s (`PAUSED_RECHECK_MS`), so a paused business costs one check per 5 s per worker and the indexer never spins on rows it may not embed. Its rows are embedded once the business may again (a top-up, a grant, the override cleared, a new period). No new column or state. | Task notes ("leave rows pending while the business is paused"); default: no schema change, nothing to re-arm when the pause ends. |
| 9 | Backfills (`memory.backfill`) and reindexes (`models.policy.changed`) only mark rows pending, so the indexer's check covers them: a paused business's backfill adds rows that wait. | Task notes; A8 decision 15. |
| 10 | **Recording.** After each embedding call that answers: one use of `embedding_tokens`, quantity the tokens the provider reported (`usage.tokens`), variant the model's ref. Recall: `source` `memory.recall`, the reader as `subject` (`user:<id>` / `contact:<id>`), the turn's `runId` and channel (the principal's on the API). Indexer: one use per business per batch, `source` `memory.index`, channel `background`, no subject. `record` never throws. A call that fails or is aborted reports no usage and records nothing. | Task notes ("Record usage.tokens from embed and embedMany"); A3 decision 16. |
| 11 | A provider that reports **no usage** (the AI SDK then gives `NaN`) is charged an estimate: one token per four characters of each text, at least one per text, rounded up. Undercounting would make embeddings free on such a gateway. | Default, fail closed (charge something rather than nothing); A3 decision 23 uses the same estimate for the scripted model. |
| 12 | A conversation message is still indexed with the vector its turn's recall made: no second embedding and no second use. | A8 decision 13. |
| 13 | The credits screen names the meter in every language (`credits.meter.embedding_tokens`: "Memory search tokens"); `GET /api/credits` lists it like every meter. | A3 decision 26. |
| 14 | The sample prices embeddings through its `MODEL_PROVIDERS` (`gateway/embed` has a price) plus the markup; `.env.example` says so. Nothing in the framework names a price. | Boundary rule (prices are the application's). |

## Assumptions to review

- **Recall is refused at the pause like a turn.** Recall is a read, but it is a paid model call.
  Should a paused business still recall (only the indexer stopped), or should recall be refused
  only by the budget and not by an `embedding_tokens` limit?
- **One overshoot per batch.** The indexer checks before a batch of up to 50 texts, so one batch can
  take a business past its cap, as one turn can (A3 decision 14). Is that bound acceptable?
- **Embeddings estimated at four characters a token** when the gateway reports nothing. Should the
  deployment be able to set the ratio, or refuse such a gateway?
- **Embedding price defaults to the model's own price plus the markup.** Should embeddings be free
  to businesses unless the operator prices them (`embedding_tokens`)?
- **5 seconds between checks of a paused business** in the indexer. Indexing resumes at most that
  long (plus the 10 s settings cache in another process) after a top-up or the override is
  cleared. Is that fast enough?

## API sketch

```ts
// @hykr/api, credits/meters.ts
FrameworkMeters.embeddingTokens   // defineMeter({ name: 'embedding_tokens', unit: 'token', per: 1_000_000, fallbackPrice })
interface MeterDef { …; fallbackPrice?(variant, plan): number | undefined }

// @hykr/api, metering.ts
class UsageRefused extends Error { refusal: UsageRefusal }

// @hykr/api, memory/recall.service.ts
RecallService.embed(tenantId, text, { timeoutMs?, subject?, channel?, runId? })
  // check embedding_tokens → embed → record; throws ModelRefused | UsageRefused | Error

// HTTP
GET /api/memory/recall?q=…   // 402 { error: 'credits_paused', rule, reason } while paused

// Prices (CREDITS_CONFIG.prices or the plan's prices)
"embedding_tokens": 40, "embedding_tokens.gateway/embed": 25   // credits per million tokens
// limits
"limits": { "embedding_tokens": 2000000 }
```

## Test plan

- Unit (`credits.test.ts`): the `embedding_tokens` meter pauses on the budget, the operator's pause
  and its own limit, not on a `tokens` limit; its fallback price is the model's own input price
  plus the markup in the credits currency, nothing in another currency or for an unknown model.
- Unit (`memory.test.ts`): reported tokens win; none reported is a token per four characters, at
  least one per text.
- E2E (`credits.e2e.test.ts`, a business of its own):
  - recall through the API is metered (quantity, variant `fake/embedding`, source
    `memory.recall`, the reader as subject) and priced by the plan's `embedding_tokens.<model>`;
  - a turn's recall is metered with the turn's run id;
  - a memory written is embedded by the indexer and metered (`memory.index`);
  - paused by the operator: `GET /api/memory/recall` is 402 `credits_paused` /
    `credits_suspended` for a member and for the assistant, nothing is recorded; a memory written
    while paused stays `pending` (no attempt counted) while another business's memory is
    embedded; once the override is cleared it is embedded and metered;
  - a limit on `embedding_tokens`: recall is 402 `usage_limit_reached`, a turn still runs, without
    recall and with no embedding recorded.
- Existing credits tests that counted a turn's uses now expect its recall embedding too.

## Deferred

- Metering the guardrail's model calls and WhatsApp voice-note transcription (developer guide
  section 14, "Metering everything"): not embeddings, not S4's.
- Charging for embedding calls that fail or are aborted: the provider reports no usage for them.
- Refusing recall per person (a rate limit per principal rather than per IP): the cap now bounds
  what a business spends; a per-person limit is a separate decision.
