# B9: share links and public surfaces

## Summary

A business shows something to someone who has no account, or who is not signed in: a record (an
order, with its lines, its PDF and the PDF's acceptance), a saved page, or a form that creates a
record. B9 gives the framework the mechanism; what can be shared, what a form asks and what it
creates are the application's.

- **Share links** (`app.share_links`, `framework/db/src/schema/share-links.ts`). A link names one
  target: a record, a saved page, or a public form. It is expiring (always: the business's
  `shares.defaultDays`, at most `shares.maxDays`), revocable, optionally limited in uses, and
  optionally **gated by a one-time code** sent to the link's recipient (a WhatsApp contact or an
  email address the business gave). Only the token's SHA-256 is stored: the link (`/share/<token>`)
  is shown once, when it is made.
- **The guest principal.** A request to `/api/share/:token/…` passes `ShareGuard`
  (`framework/core/src/http/share.guard.ts`), which turns the token (and, for a gated link, the
  session its code opened) into a principal: actor `guest` (the link's id), channel `share`,
  assurance `whatsapp_number` (`otp` once the link's code is entered), no roles and no capability.
  It never reaches the generated routes (they still need a session or an agent identity), only the
  share routes, which read and write through the same repositories, projections and `runWrite`.
- **The `share` grant** (`@hykr/policy` `accessFor`, `core/plugins/share.ts`). A guest reads only
  what its link names: the record, and the child records its shareable declares, while the link is
  live (checked in SQL on every query: not revoked, not expired). Field policy and read projectors
  apply as to anyone holding no capability: every restricted field stays hidden.
- **Shareables and public forms are declared by the application**: `defineShareable({ entity,
  children, documents, acceptances, pages, view, otp, maxDays })` and `definePublicForm({ name,
  entity, label, fields, custom, fixed, customerName, otp })`, contributed as `shareables` and
  `publicForms`. Nothing is shareable until a module says so.
- **Public views.** The share page renders the application's own views with the data the share
  route returned (`ViewDataProvider` in `@hykr/app`, read by `InlineView`), with actions switched
  off. A shared page renders its blocks the same way.
- **A public form** creates one record of the declared entity, through the entity's own create
  operation in `runWrite`, as the guest: owned by the business's default owner, with the link's
  customer (or a new customer record for an anonymous submission). The questions are the
  application's: the fields of the entity's create schema it lists, in its words (`fieldLabels`),
  plus the business's own custom fields on that entity (A11), which a business adds without a
  deploy.
- **In the app**: `sharePlugin()` serves `/share/:token` on its own, signed in or not (the public
  route gate in `AppShell`), with the business's branding; `<ShareLinks>` makes, lists and revokes
  a record's, a page's or a form's links.

The sample: the orders module declares its orders shareable (with their lines, their PDF and its
acceptance). A new `briefs` module holds a brief questionnaire (`briefs.brief`): a business sends
its link (or posts it), and each answer is a brief record the team reads.

## Assumptions to review

- **Every link expires.** At most 90 days by default; a form posted on a website must be linked
  again when its link ends. Should a business be able to publish a form without an end date?
- **Who may share.** Anyone on the team (owner, admin, ops, staff) who reaches a record may share
  it; revoking others' links is for owners and admins. Should sharing need its own role, or an
  approval (a level) for some records?
- **A page is shared as its maker sees it.** A shared page shows its blocks with the maker's reach
  but none of their capabilities. Is that the right reading of "share my page", or should pages
  be shared only when every block is about one shared record?
- **An anonymous submission makes a customer record.** A web form with no recipient creates a
  customer per submission (named by the form's name field), like an unknown WhatsApp sender. Should
  submissions be matched to existing customers (by phone), or wait as leads without a customer?
- **One recipient per link.** A link has one recipient (the code's destination and the signer).
  Should a family share one link with several numbers?
- **Signing needs a WhatsApp contact.** A guest signs only as a contact (the recipient's number);
  an email-only recipient can open a gated link but cannot accept. Should email recipients sign
  too (a signer kind for guests in the evidence)?
- **No assistant.** The assistant can neither make nor send a link. Should "send Asha the order
  link" be a tool (it would then need an approval level)?
- **Links are not sent.** The person making a link copies it; the framework does not send it on
  WhatsApp or by email. Should making a link optionally send it to the recipient (a template)?
- **A session lasts 12 hours.** After the code, a device sees the link for 12 hours (or until the
  link ends). Is that right for a client page opened over several days?

## API sketch

```ts
// A module's index.ts: contributes: { shareables: [...], publicForms: [...] }
import { definePublicForm, defineShareable } from '@hykr/contracts';

export const OrderShare = defineShareable({
  entity: 'order',
  children: ['orderItem'],          // child entities that come with it
  documents: ['orders.order'],      // PDFs a guest may open
  acceptances: true,                // and accept (B8), as the link's contact
});

export const BriefForm = definePublicForm({
  name: 'briefs.brief',
  entity: 'brief',
  label: { en: 'Tell us what you need', hi: '…', te: '…' },
  fields: ['name', 'phone', 'summary', 'budget', 'neededBy', 'notes'],
  customerName: 'name',             // names the customer an anonymous answer creates
  fixed: { source: 'form' },
});
```

```
Members (people only)
GET  /api/shares/kinds                         → { entities: ShareableInfo[], forms: PublicFormInfo[] }
GET  /api/shares?entity=order&id=… | ?page=… | ?form=briefs.brief → { items: ShareLinkInfo[] }
POST /api/shares { target: { entity, id } | { page } | { form }, expiresInDays?, otp?, to?, maxUses?, label? }
                                               → { status: 'done', value: ShareLinkInfo & { token, path, url } }
POST /api/shares/:id/revoke                    → { status: 'done', value: ShareLinkInfo }

Guests (no session; x-share-session after a code)
GET  /api/share/:token                         → Shared { link, business, gate, record? | page? | form? }
POST /api/share/:token/code { lang? }          → { status: 'done', value: { id, channel, sentTo, expiresAt } }
POST /api/share/:token/verify { challengeId, code } → { status: 'done', value: { session, expiresAt } }
GET  /api/share/:token/documents/:documentId   → DocumentInfo with url
GET  /api/share/:token/acceptances/documents/:documentId → AcceptanceStatus
POST /api/share/:token/acceptances/challenges  → B8's challenge, as the guest
POST /api/share/:token/acceptances             → B8's accept, as the guest
POST /api/share/:token/submit { values, custom? } → { status: 'done', value: { entity, id } }
```

```tsx
// The app
<AppShell plugins={[settingsPlugin(), sharePlugin(), …]} />   // serves /share/:token
<ShareLinks target={{ entity: 'order', id }} />                // a member's links on a record
<ShareLinks target={{ form: 'briefs.brief' }} />               // a form's links
<ViewDataProvider value={{ readOnly: true, data: (key) => … }}><InlineView dataKey="record" … /></ViewDataProvider>
```

## Test plan

- `framework/policy/src/share.test.ts`: a guest reads only by the `share` grant, writes nothing
  (except a form submitter's customer create), and a page guest reads with the maker's roles.
- `framework/core/src/plugins/share.test.ts` (contracts has no test runner): form fields from a
  create schema (types, required, labels, choices, money), refusals of unsupported fields and
  missing required ones.
- `framework/api/test/framework/share.e2e.test.ts` (fixture `share`: agreements with clauses, an
  inquiry form): a member makes a record link (token once, hash stored); a customer cannot
  (403), another business's member gets 404 for the record; the guest reads the record with its
  clauses, never the margin; another record, an undeclared entity and the generated routes are
  out of reach; revoked and ended links are 410; a gated link answers only its gate until the
  emailed code (wrong code spends an attempt), then the session reads it, and another link's
  session does not; the guest opens the PDF (URL) and not another record's; a WhatsApp contact
  recipient accepts the PDF by B8's code (evidence channel `share`, assurance `otp`); a page link
  shows blocks with the maker's reach, without their capability, and omits undeclared entities;
  a form link creates an inquiry owned by the default owner for the link's customer, audited as
  the guest, custom fields included, `maxUses` enforced, bad input 400, and a create that would
  park refused `guest_cannot_wait` (audited); deleting a record revokes its links.
- `framework/core/src/plugins/share.test.ts`: the form-field description (above).
- `application/api/test/domain/share.e2e.test.ts`: an approved order shared with its customer
  (`recordCustomer`), behind a WhatsApp code: the guest sees the order and its lines without
  cost, opens the PDF and accepts it as the customer's contact; the brief form's link asks the
  brief's questions, and an answer is a brief in the business's currency, owned by the business,
  for a new customer, out of staff's reach.

## Deferred

- SSR, SEO, link previews (decision 25).
- Sending a link (WhatsApp template, email) and an assistant tool to make one.
- WhatsApp turns carrying share links (`RenderTurn.pages`).
- Email-only signers (decision 14), several recipients per link.
- Comments by guests (B16), files uploaded by guests (a form asks no file yet).
- A per-business custom domain for share pages (A11's domains are not read for share URLs).
- Links without an end date (decision 5).
- Privacy (B18): a link's recipient email stays on the link until the business is deleted;
  erasing a contact clears its links' contact (the foreign key) but not their email. A
  privacy contributor for `app.share_links` belongs with B18.
- Agent tools for share links (decision 4), and links in WhatsApp replies.
