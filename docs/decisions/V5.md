# V5: credits say when a business is paused, and a refused send uses no quota

## Summary

Fixes from the live validation of A3 (`VALIDATION.md`, defects C5-6, C5-3, C5-1, C5-2 and C5-4).

- **A refused marketing send no longer uses up the contact's weekly cap (C5-6).** The cap
  (`general.marketingPerWeek`) counted every marketing row of the last 7 days, whatever its
  status. A message a credits pause refused (recorded `failed`, never sent) took the contact's
  one send of the week, and the approved resend after the top-up was dropped. The cap now counts
  only messages that went out or are on their way (`queued`, `sent`, `delivered`, `read`).
- **`GET /api/credits` says the assistant is paused whenever turns are refused (C5-3).** `paused`
  was set only for the budget or an operator pause, so a reached `tokens` or `tool_calls` limit,
  which refuses every turn and every agent call, read `paused: null`. The status, the turn hook
  and the agent guard now share one definition of "turns are refused" (`turnRefusal`). Each meter
  also says why it is paused (`refusal`), and the app's credits screen shows it.
- **`alertAt` must be below `pauseAt` (C5-1).** An alert at the pause is never early. The check
  was `alertAt > pauseAt`; it is now `alertAt >= pauseAt`, for the business's threshold, the
  operator's plan and `CREDITS_CONFIG.defaults` (which had no check at all).
- **A member who may not buy credits gets 403 before anything else (C5-2).** The purchase's
  `load` checked the deployment's currency and purchase provider before `authorize` ran, so on a
  deployment without a provider (the sample's) staff got 503 and no denial was audited. The same
  order made a staff member's bad alert threshold a 400 instead of a 403.
- **The sample words a meter's limit apart from the credits (C5-4).** Every cap's notice said
  "the assistant is paused. Add more in the app", also when only memory search or voice had
  stopped, and adding credits never lifts a limit. Two new templates (`limit_low_v1`,
  `limit_reached_v1`) say what stopped and that it starts again next period, in en, hi and te.

## Decisions

| 3 | A marketing send the cap drops is logged with the count and the cap (`marketing cap reached for contact <id>: <n> of <cap> this week`), before the "no channel could reach" warning. | Validation C5-6 (verifier's optional sketch). |
| 4 | **Turns are refused** has one definition: `TURN_METERS` (`tokens`, then `tool_calls`) and `turnRefusal(a)`, the first refusal among them (`budget.ts`). | 5 | `CreditsStatus.paused` is `turnRefusal(a)`: the operator's pause, the budget, or a `tokens` or `tool_calls` limit (`rule: 'usage_limit_reached'`, `cap` the meter). | 7 | The app's credits screen shows, under each meter whose limit is reached, its reason (`credits.pausedNow`, an existing key in every language). The banner (from `paused`) now also covers turn limits. | Validation C5-3 (verifier's sketch, step 4). |

| 10 | A purchase is authorized before the deployment's set-up is looked at: `buyCredits.load` resolves the currency and the provider only for a principal `manager()` allows. Anyone else gets `authorize`'s 403 (`credits_manage`, `people_only` or `needs_assurance`), audited `denied`. A manager still gets 503 `credits_not_configured` / `purchases_unavailable` or 400 `unknown_provider` / `provider_required`, from `load`, before anything can park. | A3 decisions 21 and 22; validation C5-2 (verifier's sketch). |
| 11 | The alerts route's 400 precheck (`planProblems`) runs only for a principal `manager()` allows (`mayManageCredits`); anyone else goes straight to `runWrite`, which denies (403) and audits. | Validation C5-2 (the verifier's related case). |
| 12 | Sample: `credits_low_v1` and `credits_out_v1` (and their email subjects) stay the words for the **budget** (`cap: 'credits'`), unchanged (templates are versioned). A meter's limit gets its own notifications, `account.limit_low` and `account.limit_reached`, with the templates `limit_low_v1` (`what`, `percent`) and `limit_reached_v1` (`what`, `stopped`), category `utility`. Neither says to add credits: a limit starts again next period. | Validation C5-4; A3 decision 9 (a limit pauses "whatever the credits"); CLAUDE.md (templates are versioned forever). |

In en, hi and te. | A3 decision 9; S4 decisions 2 and 14. |

## Assumptions to review

- **Provider failures leave the cap.** Decision 2 stops counting a message the provider failed,
  not only one a pause refused. If a business wants a failed marketing send (a number not on
  WhatsApp, say) to count, the query should exclude only rows with `meta.refused`.
- **The operator is held to `alertAt < pauseAt` too.** A3 decision 6 names the business's own
  threshold; the operator's plan and the deployment's defaults now get the same check, because
  an alert at the pause is never early for anyone. A deployment whose `CREDITS_CONFIG.defaults`
  sets `pauseAt: 80` and no `alertAt` now fails at startup (80 is not below 80).
- **"It starts again next period."** The sample's limit notices say the limit resets next
  period. The operator can also raise it, but the sample does not name the operator to a
  business.

## Test plan

`framework/api/test/framework/credits.e2e.test.ts`:

- WhatsApp: a marketing template sent by hand while the business pauses marketing is recorded
  failed (`credits_exhausted`) and not sent; after the top-up the same send to that contact is
  delivered and metered; a third is still dropped at the cap of 1, checked once its dispatch job
  has finished (C5-6).
- Limits: a tool-call limit reports `paused: { rule: 'usage_limit_reached', cap: 'tool_calls' }`
  to the business and to the operator, and its meter carries the refusal; a tokens limit does
  the same; an embedding-token limit leaves `paused` null with its meter's refusal; the operator's
  `run` override shows no meter paused (C5-3).
- Alerts: `alertAt` equal to `pauseAt` is 400, for the business and the operator; a staff
  member's out-of-range threshold is 403, audited (C5-1, C5-2).
- Purchases: staff naming no provider (two are set up) or one that does not exist get 403,
  audited, not 400; the owner still gets 400 `provider_required` (C5-2).

`framework/api/test/framework/credits.test.ts` (unit): `CREDITS_CONFIG.defaults` with
`alertAt >= pauseAt` is refused; on a deployment with no purchase provider, the assistant's

## Same-class sites checked

- `NotifyService.tapped` reads the latest row of a notification to a contact whatever its
  status. A refused row keeps the notification's `meta` (`row`, `resourceType`), so a tap still
  finds the record; with `replyTo`, only the delivered row (which has a provider id) matches. No
  change.
- The OTP rate limit (`otp.service.ts`) counts challenges, sent or not: that limit is against
  abuse, and codes are never paused by credits. No change.
- The `messages` meter records a send only when the provider takes it (`markSent`); a refused
  row is never metered (existing test). Email rows carry no category; the cap is WhatsApp's.
- `currencyOrFail` in the operator's grant `load`, and the operator's plan precheck, run before
  authorization too, but only behind the operator guard (`platform.credits`), so nobody without
  the right reaches them. No change.
- The sample's other message counts (`application/`) are tests, not caps.

## Deferred

- The framework's paused sentence (`credits.paused`, said by a refused turn and by a run that
  stops mid-way) says the business "has used its credits" and someone can add more, also when a
  `tokens` or `tool_calls` limit refused the turn. Saying "a usage limit is reached" needs the
  refusal's rule in `EndWhenPaused` (`@hykr/agents`).
- Telling the person who approved a marketing send that the cap dropped it: today it is only
  logged (decision 3).
- `limit_low_v1` and `limit_reached_v1` must be registered (`pnpm templates:sync`) on every
  deployment; until they are approved, the limit notices go by email.
