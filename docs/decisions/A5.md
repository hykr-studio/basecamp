# A5: approval levels on the payload, named approvers, level-1 undo

## Summary

The closed tier union (`0 | 1 | 2 | 3`) is gone. The application declares its own approval levels
(`defineApprovalLevel({ name, rank, label, policy })`, contributed as `approvalLevels`). Each level
has a policy: whether it parks, an undo window, who decides, where and how surely, and how long it
waits. A decision names a level by name. The framework only compares ranks (the highest wins) and
keys undo on `undoMs`. Without levels, everything works as before.

Which actions sit at which level is declared by the application too: level rules
(`defineLevelRule({ name, on, decide })`, contributed as `levelRules`) see the operation, its
validated input, the loaded row and the business's settings. Thresholds and approvers per amount
band are per-business settings (the `approvals` section). Approvers are chosen by capability
(`approvals.decide`, `marketing.approve`, or the application's own), never by role name. Nobody
decides a request they made unless the policy sends it back to them (the person deciding their own
assistant's request). Pending approvals expire on a schedule. A write that runs with an undo window
can be undone by the person it was for, through `POST /api/undo/:id`, until the window closes: the
change is reversed through the write path and its held events are withdrawn. The app shows the
level and amount on approval cards, hides the decision from someone who may not make it, and has
`/approvals` and `/approvals/:id` (the deep link WhatsApp answers point to).

The sample application declares the four levels of its product brief (read, internal write that can
be undone, a send to someone outside the business, and money or irreversible actions decided by a
named person in the app, signed in), maps its to-do writes to `internal` and `notify.send` to
`external`. The `orders` module (B3B5) parks an order above the business's limit at
`irreversible`, with its amount, for holders of its own capability `orders.approve`; the
assistant's save and cancel of an order wait for the person, without a level (#30).

## Assumptions to review

- **Level 2 for a person's own send.** The sample's `external` level parks only when an assistant or
  a background job sends; a person sending themselves goes ahead (they are the human the PRD asks
  for). Should a person's own send to someone outside also wait, and for whom?
- **Who loosens.** A business may switch off parking only for levels the application marks
  `tenantMayLoosen` (the sample: `external`). Should owners be able to loosen any level?
- **No threshold set.** `overThreshold` counts a missing threshold as exceeded, so a new business
  parks every amount until it sets one. Should a missing threshold mean "never parks" instead?
- **Named approver who asked.** When the band's named approver is the requester, the request goes to
  holders of `approvals.decide` other than them. Should it go to the next band up, or to the owner?
- **Self-approval everywhere.** An owner alone in a business can no longer approve their own
  marketing send or a customer-facing request the policy sends to a capability. Is a single-person
  business allowed to approve its own requests?
- **Overriding the framework capabilities.** An application can give more roles `approvals.decide`
  but cannot take it from `owner` or `ops`; it names its own capability on its levels instead. Is
  that enough, or should a business be able to remove the default holders?
- **Undo window length.** The sample's internal level gives 10 seconds. What should the product's
  window be, and should it differ per channel (WhatsApp replies are slower)?
- **Undo of a delete.** Rows removed by cascade with the deleted row are not put back. Should an
  entity with children refuse undo instead?
- **Approval list for the requester.** The requester can open their own request read-only (deep
  link). Should they also see a list of everything they are waiting on?
- **Sends in an undoable write.** Done in H1: outbox sends and notifications (now scheduled from
  the stored events) wait for the undo window, and an undo withdraws them (`docs/decisions/H1.md`).
- **The assistant cannot undo.** A person undoes their assistant's write in the app; there is no
  tool for "undo that" on WhatsApp. Should there be one?
- **Live updates wait for the undo window.** A to-do the assistant adds shows on the person's
  other open screens 10 seconds later (#31). Is that delay acceptable, or should live signals go
  out at once with a second signal when a write is undone?
- **Orders and level rules.** The orders module names `irreversible` on its own over-limit
  decision instead of contributing a level rule, so an order's save under the limit has no level
  and no undo window (#30). Should orders under the limit be `internal` and undoable?

## Deferred

- Multi-step levels (two approvers). The policy has one decider per request.
- Delegation and escalation when a named approver is away, beyond the capability fallback.
- Push and email for approval requests: done in H1 (push, WhatsApp where it can decide, email,
  and always the in-app inbox; `docs/decisions/H1.md`).
- Holding back `app.outbox` sends and notification scheduling inside an undo window: done in H1.
- A requester's list of their own pending requests.
- An assistant tool to undo (no new tools in this unit, so no scripted-model scripts).
- Tool descriptions that say a write may wait because of its level (`tool-factory.ts` is A7's).
- Tenant-defined roles (A4) will need `Recipient { capability }` expansion (`rolesWith`) to read
  the business's own role table.
