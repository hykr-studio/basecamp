# V11: events, credits, words and auth, what the reviews left

## Summary

The reviews of V5 (credits), V6 (retries, events, egress), V7 (words and channels) and V9 (auth
routes, OAuth pages, verified domains) approved those units and left findings. This unit fixes
the defects among them, the same-class sites, two hazards in `framework/core/src/write/approvals.ts`
(an approver who left the business, and a replay that no longer validates), and the doc
corrections and missing tests the reviewers named.

Work in progress: the sections below are filled in as each fix lands.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A named approver who left gives way to the business.** By default, when a request's named approver (`approverUserId`: an amount band's person, a rule's `{ userId }`, or the requester deciding their own) is no longer on the business's team, the holders of `approvals.decide` decide it, never the requester. "On the team" is the rule a request parks under: a membership in the business with a role no outsider is given, among the roles the business has (`teamRoles(rolesOfBusiness(settings))`). So a person who left, and one who holds only a contact's or participant's roles now, have both left; back on the team, the request is theirs alone again. | Brief (V9's approver-left case, "record this as a decision"); V9 Deferred; default (the park-time rule, applied again at decide time). |
| 2 | The rule is one fact read in two places. `decidable` (lists, `get`, `decide`) gains an arm for holders of `approvals.decide` over rows whose named approver has no team membership (`NOT EXISTS` on `app.memberships` with `roles && <team roles>`), and the named approver's own arm now needs that membership too. `mayDecide(row, p, { approverGone })` and the card's `decider` (`{ by: 'capability', capability: 'approvals.decide' }`) take the same fact, which `ApprovalService` looks up for the rows it returns (`goneApprovers`, one query). `keepOwn` (WhatsApp) still calls `mayDecide` without it: a request it renders has just parked, when its named approver is on the team by construction. | Advisor (the sync `mayDecide` feeds `canDecide` and the 403 `own_request`); default. |
| 3 | **A replay that no longer validates ends as failed.** `runWrite` validates first (`op.input.parse`), which throws a `ZodError`, not an `HttpException`, so a stored input that lost a required field (an erasure's scrubbing, V3's hazard) answered 500 and stayed pending until it expired. `decide` now closes it as `failed` with "What was asked is no longer complete (<paths>)". A replay refused with 400, 410 or 422 also ends as failed (it would be refused again), joining 401, 403, 404 and 409; a 5xx or a 429 still leaves it pending. | Brief (V3's replay hazard: "have decide close a replay that fails validation as failed"). |
