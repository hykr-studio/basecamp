# A11X: exporting a business's data

## Summary

A person allowed to (the capability `data.export`, held by `owner` unless the application or the
business grants it to another role) exports the business's data from the app or the back office.
`POST /api/exports` is a command (`export.start`): it goes through `runWrite` (validated,
idempotent, authorized, audited) and starts a **job** (B11) of the kind `export.data`. The worker
reads every served entity **as the person who asked**, through each entity's scoped repository and
`toRead(row, p)`: their access, record grants and field hiding apply exactly as on
`GET /api/<plural>`. It writes one JSON file and one CSV file per entity (and per contributed
export source), plus a `manifest.json`, into one zip archive, and keeps the archive on the job as
a B1 file. Only the person who started the export reaches the job and its file; they lose the file
when they lose `data.export`.

The app gets `exportPlugin` (a `/export` screen and its destination, shown to holders of
`data.export`); the back office's Settings screen gets a "Data export" section. The sample
application lists the plugin; its e2e test exports a business as its owner, and as a member of a
business-made role who may export but not see what to-dos cost.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One framework feature, `framework/api/src/features/export/` (`exportFeature`), with no table of its own: an export is a job (`app.jobs`, B11) whose result is a file (`app.files`, B1). It reserves `/api/exports`. | Plan A11 row ("Export runs as a B11 job writing B1 files"); task notes ("features/export: a B11 job writing B1 files"). |
| 2 | Who may export is a capability, `data.export` (`DataExportCapability` in `@hykr/contracts`), held by the built-in `owner` only. It is grantable: an application gives it to its roles (`roleOverrides`, a role's `capabilities`), and a business to its own roles (A4). Starting asks `can(p, 'data.export')`, never a role name; the refusal is 403 `needs_data_export`, audited as denied. | Task ("owner/capability gated"; "export a business (owner only)"); CLAUDE.md (check capabilities, never role names); default: the narrowest built-in holder, fail closed. |
| 3 | Starting is a command, `export.start` (`StartExportSpec`, `POST /api/exports`), `expose: 'human'`: people only, no assistant tool. Its `run` takes a transaction-scoped advisory lock on the business, refuses a second export while one of the business is queued or running (409 `export_running`), checks `only` (400 `unknown_export_part`) and calls `startJob`. The command's audit row (`export.start`, resource type `tenant`, the business's id) and the job's (`job.start`, `job.submit`, `job.succeeded` or `job.failed`) are the export's audit trail. | CLAUDE.md rule 1 (one write path); B11 decision 3 (a job starts inside a write); default: the assistant does not take a whole business's data out (WhatsApp and voice are weaker channels), and one export at a time per business bounds the worker's load. |
| 4 | Input: `formats` (`json`, `csv`; both by default) and `only` (entity names and export source names; everything by default). | Task ("JSON/CSV per entity"); default. |
| 5 | **What is read.** Every entity the process serves (`entityDefs()`), sorted by plural. Skipped, and named in the manifest's `skipped`: an entity whose list is `expose: 'internal'` (`not_listed`: people cannot list it either), one switched off for the business by a feature flag (`switched_off`, A11's `FeatureGates`, failing closed), and one whose plugins refuse the person's list (`refused`, e.g. a default sort on a field hidden from them). Rows of another business never appear: the scoped repository already keeps them out, and a platform-shared row (A11 shared records, `tenant_id` of the platform's business) is left out because it is not this business's data (a business's override of one is its own row and is exported). | Task ("field hiding applied for the requester"); CLAUDE.md ("Every read leaves through `def.toRead(row, p)`"); A11 decisions 5, 15, 16. |
| 6 | **As whom.** The worker reads as the person who started the export, as they stand when the export runs (`ctx.starter()`, refreshed like an approval's replay): still in the business, today's roles, today's capabilities. Someone who lost `data.export` meanwhile gets a failed job (`needs_data_export`); someone who left the business, `starter_unavailable`. Their access (`own`, `customer`, `tenant`, record grants) scopes each list and `toRead` projects each row (entity plugins' `project`, field policy, read projectors), so the archive holds exactly what the person could page through on `GET /api/<plural>`. | Task ("field hiding applied for the requester"); B11 decision 4 (`starter()`); fail closed. |
| 7 | **Export sources.** Data that is not an entity (a feature's or module's own tables) joins the export through a registry, `dataExportSources` (contributed as `dataExportSources`): `{ name, rows(ctx) }`, where `rows` yields the JSON-ready rows the principal may read. Each row then passes the read projectors with the source's name as its resource type, so a projector can hide a source's fields too. A source name must be lower case letters, digits and `-`, and may not repeat another source, an entity's plural or `manifest` (the start stops). The framework contributes no source yet. | Plan F0 (export contributors, here per business rather than per data subject); default. |
| 8 | **Files.** One zip archive, `export-<UTC date>.zip` (`application/zip`; the business is in its manifest, and only its person reaches it), holding `manifest.json`, `<plural>.json` and `<plural>.csv` per entity (`<name>.json`/`.csv` per source). JSON: an array, one record per line, as the API returns it. CSV (RFC 4180): UTF-8 with a byte-order mark (spreadsheets then read Hindi and Telugu), CRLF line ends, a header of the read schema's fields in order (a field hidden on every row is left out); objects and arrays as JSON in their cell; a text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a carriage return gets a leading `'` (spreadsheet formula injection), only in the CSV. One file instead of one per entity keeps a business with many entities within the job's result-file limit and gives people one download. The zip is written by the framework (`node:zlib` deflate and CRC-32), with no new dependency; `readZip` reads one back. | Task ("JSON/CSV per entity"); OWASP CSV injection guidance; default. |
| 9 | **Manifest.** `{ version: 1, tenantId, exportedAt, exportedBy: { kind, id }, formats, parts: [{ name, kind: 'entity' \| 'source', module, label, rows, files }], skipped: [{ name, reason }] }` (`ExportManifest` in `@hykr/contracts`). The job's output (`ExportOutput`) holds the counts (`rows`, `bytes`, `parts`, `skipped`), without file names. | Default. |
| 10 | **Size.** Each part is compressed as soon as it is read, and the archive is checked against `FILES_MAX_BYTES` (B1's cap on every stored file) as it grows: past it, the export stops and fails `too_large`, naming the limit. Rows of one entity are held in memory while it is written. | B1 decision 12; default: fail early instead of running out of memory. |
| 11 | **Time.** The kind's deadline is 60 minutes and one run may take 55; reading stops when the run's signal aborts. A run that throws is tried once more (`submitAttempts: 2`), then fails `submit_failed`. These are technical limits, not business values. | B11 decision 9 (a kind declares its deadline); default. |
| 12 | **Who reaches the archive.** The export job is about no record, so only the person it was started for reaches it and its file (B11 decision 11); another business gets 404. A read projector (`export.results`) also hides an export job's `output` and `files` from anyone without `data.export` now (system principals excepted), so someone who loses the capability loses the archives they made: `GET /api/files/:id` is 404 for them (B11 decision 13 checks the job as projected). | Task ("owner/capability gated"); fail closed. |
| 13 | **Progress** is reported per part (`Exporting <plural>`, 0–90 %), then the job succeeds with the message `Ready`. These are the job's own technical messages, like B11's, not framework phrases. | B11 decision 4 (`ctx.progress`). |
| 14 | **App.** `exportPlugin` (`@hykr/app`): the `/export` route and an "Export data" destination, hidden unless `/api/me` lists `data.export`. `ExportScreen` lets the person pick the formats, start an export and see their exports (`useExports()`, `<JobProgress>` with the archive through `<FileThumbs>`). Its words are framework phrases (`export.*`, en/hi/te). `useStartExport()` and `useExports()` are exported for an application's own screen. | Task ("app/back-office trigger"); F2 plugin slots. |
| 15 | **Back office.** The Settings screen shows a "Data export" section to holders of `data.export`: start, see progress, download the archive (`api.files.get(id).url`). | Task ("app/back-office trigger"); coverage checklist (export on the desktop setup screens). |
| 16 | **The sample.** The application lists `exportPlugin`. Who may export stays the framework's default (owners). Its e2e test exports a business as the owner and as a business-made `exporter` role given to ops, whose to-dos are exported without their cost (`todos.cost.read`). The sample's access map gives owners their own records only (`BUSINESS_ACCESS`), so an owner's export holds what the owner reads; the export never widens anyone's reach. | Task ("Sample demo: export a business (owner only)"); boundary rule (who reads what is the application's). |
| 17 | **Personal data.** An export's job is about no record and started for its person, so "delete my data" erases it and its archive (B11 decision 18). | B11 decision 18. |

## Assumptions to review

| Topic | What we chose | Question for the product owner |
|---|---|---|
| Who exports | Owners only by default; the application or the business can grant `data.export` to other roles | Should admins (or an accounts role) export by default? Should an export need a second person's approval? |
| What an owner's export holds | Exactly what the person can list in the app, under the application's access map (the sample's owners read their own records only) | Should a business export always hold every record of the business, whoever asks (a separate "whole business" reach for exports), or stay as the person sees it? |
| The assistant | Cannot start an export (no tool); it can read the job with `get-job` | Should "export my data" work from the assistant, and on which channels? |
| Format | One zip with JSON and CSV per entity and a manifest | Do businesses need other formats (Excel, an accounting package's import format), or separate files per entity? |
| Files on records | The archive lists file ids found on records; it does not include the files' bytes | Should photos and documents on records be in the export, given their size? |
| Retention | Archives stay until "delete my data" (B11), or until the `jobs.files` retention period (V8; the sample: 30 days) | How long should an export archive be kept (a day, a week)? |
| Notification | None: the screen shows progress | Should the person be told (push, email) when the export is ready? |
| Downloads | Downloading the archive is not audited (the start and the result are) | Must every download of an export be audited? |
| One at a time | One unfinished export per business | Is one at a time per business right, or per person? |
| Size | `FILES_MAX_BYTES` (25 MiB by default, compressed) | What is the largest business expected, and should a large export be split into several archives? |

## API sketch

```ts
// @hykr/contracts
DataExportCapability          // 'data.export', roles: ['owner']
DATA_EXPORT_JOB               // 'export.data'
ExportFormat                  // 'json' | 'csv'
StartExportInput              // { formats?: ExportFormat[], only?: string[] }
StartExportSpec               // command 'export.start', POST /api/exports, expose 'human', output JobInfo
ExportPart, ExportSkipped, ExportOutput, ExportManifest

// @hykr/api
exportFeature
dataExportSources             // registry; contributes: { dataExportSources: [...] }
DataExportSource              // { name, rows(ctx: { principal, tenantId, db, signal, get }) }
DataExportJob                 // the 'export.data' kind
writeZip(entries), readZip(bytes), ZipWriter
csvOf(rows, columns), exportColumns(...)

// HTTP
POST /api/exports { formats?, only? }      → { status: 'done', value: JobInfo }   (people with data.export)
GET  /api/jobs?kind=export.data            → the person's exports (B11)
GET  /api/jobs/:id, GET /api/files/:id     → progress, the archive's URL (B11, B1)

// @hykr/app
exportPlugin, ExportScreen, useExports(), useStartExport(), useCanExport()
```

## Test plan

- `framework/api/test/framework/export.test.ts` (unit, no boot): CSV quoting, line ends, the
  byte-order mark, objects as JSON, formula injection; columns from the schema without fields
  hidden everywhere; the zip writer read back by `readZip` and by an independent reader
  (`fflate`), and `readZip` reading `fflate`'s archive; a CRC mismatch is refused; an export
  source with a bad name, the manifest's name or no `rows` stops the start.
- `framework/api/test/framework/export.e2e.test.ts` (fixture `features/export`: the fixture's
  tasks and visits, a flag that switches visits off, a task field hidden from everyone without a
  fixture capability, an export source):
  - the owner exports: 200 `done`, the job queued, `export.start` and `job.start` audited; it
    succeeds; the archive (through `GET /api/files/:id`) holds the manifest, `tasks.json` and
    `tasks.csv` with the owner's tasks only, without the hidden field, and the source's rows;
    visits are skipped (`switched_off`) until the flag is on;
  - `only` and `formats` narrow it; an unknown part is 400;
  - admin and staff are refused (403 `needs_data_export`, audited as denied); the assistant is
    refused (403);
  - another business: 404 on the job and the archive; its own export holds none of our rows;
  - a business role with `data.export` given to staff: their export holds their own tasks only;
    once the role is taken away, their archive is 404 and the job lists no files;
  - a second export while one runs: 409 `export_running`, while another business's export starts;
  - an archive over the size cap fails `too_large`.
- `application/api/test/domain/export.e2e.test.ts`: the owner exports the sample business (to-dos,
  notes, meetings and orders in the archive; another business's to-dos absent); ops are refused;
  ops given a business role with `data.export` export every to-do without its cost.

## Deferred

- Files' bytes in the archive (photos, documents on records).
- Streaming the archive to storage and splitting a large export into several archives.
- Framework export sources for framework tables (members and roles, grants, files' metadata,
  audit history, credits ledger).
- Notifying the person when the export is ready; auditing each download.
- Retention of export archives (B18's retention runner).
- An assistant tool to start an export.
- Importing an export archive back (B20 can read it with `readZip`).
